# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and protect private information shared during debugging. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through their GitHub profile rather than in a public issue.
