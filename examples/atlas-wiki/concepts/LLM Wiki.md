# LLM Wiki

The key pattern is a small semantic codebase: immutable sources inform focused wiki pages, while [[Human Review]] protects formal knowledge from silent rewrites.

## Operations

Ingest, query, and lint. See [[../sources/Karpathy Pattern]].
